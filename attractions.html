Explore India

India offers a breathtaking tapestry of heritage, nature, and adventure. From the timeless beauty of the Taj Mahal to the wild heart of its national parks, here are some must-visit destinations worth extending your trip for.

Taj Mahal

Agra, Uttar Pradesh

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the Seven Wonders of the World. This ivory-white marble mausoleum is the ultimate symbol of love and Mughal architecture.

Get Directions

Pink City — Jaipur

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Known for its stunning pink-hued architecture, Jaipur is home to the iconic Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, and the City Palace. A vibrant blend of royalty and culture.

Get Directions

Jim Corbett National Park

Nainital, Uttarakhand

India's oldest national park and a premier tiger reserve. Home to Bengal tigers, elephants, and over 600 bird species amid the Himalayan foothills.

Get Directions

Manali & Leh

Himachal Pradesh / Ladakh

The legendary Manali-Leh Highway is one of the most scenic drives on Earth. Snow-capped peaks, ancient monasteries, and pristine valleys await adventurers.

Get Directions

Ranthambore National Park

Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan

One of the best places in India to spot tigers in the wild. The ancient Ranthambore Fort looms over the jungle, creating a dramatic backdrop for safaris.

Get Directions

Explore Kerala

God's Own Country 🌴

Kerala is a lush paradise of backwaters, hill stations, beaches, and waterfalls. Whether you seek tranquility or adventure, these destinations showcase the best of this tropical wonderland.

Kerala Backwaters

Alleppey / Kumarakom

A network of serene lagoons, canals, and lakes stretching across Kerala. Experience a traditional houseboat cruise through palm-lined waterways — truly magical.

Get Directions

Wayanad

Wayanad District

A misty hill district with ancient caves, stunning waterfalls, and wildlife sanctuaries. Edakkal Caves feature prehistoric carvings dating back thousands of years.

Get Directions

Munnar

Idukki District • ~130 km from Kochi

Rolling hills blanketed with emerald tea plantations. Munnar offers misty mornings, the Eravikulam National Park (home to Nilgiri Tahr), and breathtaking viewpoints.

Get Directions

Kovalam & Varkala

Thiruvananthapuram District

Kovalam's crescent-shaped beaches and Varkala's dramatic seaside cliffs are among Kerala's most iconic coastal destinations, perfect for relaxation and sunsets.

Get Directions

Athirappilly Falls

Thrissur District • ~70 km from Kochi

The "Niagara of India" — Kerala's largest waterfall cascades 80 feet through lush rainforest. A spectacular sight during the monsoon season and a Bollywood filming favourite.

Get Directions

Explore Kochi

The Queen of the Arabian Sea

Kochi is a vibrant port city where centuries of history blend with modern energy. From the iconic Chinese Fishing Nets and heritage neighborhoods to world-class shopping, there's something for everyone just a short drive from the conference venue.

Museum of Kerala History

7 km • ~20 min drive

Features a light and sound show depicting Kerala's history and a gallery of modern art. A perfect quick cultural dive.

Get Directions

Hill Palace Museum

9 km • ~25 min drive

The largest archaeological museum in Kerala, featuring royal jewelry, palanquins, and beautiful gardens.

Get Directions

Fort Kochi Heritage Zone

23 km • ~60-90 min drive

The historic heart of Kochi. See the Chinese Fishing Nets, St. Francis Church, and colonial bungalows.

Get Directions

Marine Drive & Boating

13 km • ~40 min drive

A picturesque promenade facing the backwaters. Popular for sunset boat rides with a stunning skyline view.

Get Directions

Cherai Beach

25 km • ~50 min drive

A pristine 15-km stretch of golden sand where the backwaters meet the Arabian Sea. Known for dolphin sightings, stunning sunsets, and a calm swimming-friendly shoreline.

Get Directions

Wonderla Amusement Park

6 km • ~15 min drive

One of India's top amusement parks, located very close to Kakkanad. Clean, safe, and thrilling!

Get Directions

Lulu Mall

8 km • ~25 min drive

One of the largest shopping malls in India. Excellent for buying spices, souvenirs, or enjoying global cuisine.
